1. **Use a tidy account name.** Drive shows the owner's name to everyone who opens the link. Your dad's name is fine. If you'd rather have a brand, make a free Google account called **Big & Simple Colouring** and use that.

## Step 2: Forum posts

Before posting anywhere, **read the group's rules**. Many large parenting groups and subreddits ban self-promotion completely. If you're unsure, message the admins or mods first ("I've made some free printables for toddlers and I'm the creator. Would it be OK to share them?"). One approved post beats ten deleted ones and a ban.

Don't say the pictures were hand-drawn. If anyone asks how they were made, answer honestly.

## Step 3: Micro-influencer DM

Rules to follow:
- **Never** ask for an Amazon review in exchange for a free copy. Amazon bans incentivised reviews and can remove the book.
- In the UK, creators should mark gifted posts. Mentioning it in your message makes you look professional.

> Hi [Name]! I've loved your [specific post, e.g. "Christmas crafts reel with the paper snowflakes"]. The [specific detail] was such a good idea.
>
> I've just published a Christmas colouring book for toddlers aged 2–5, with 30 big, simple pictures and thick lines for little hands. Given your [little one / homeschool content], I thought your family might enjoy it.

Send each message by hand and personalise it. Copy-paste mass DMs get ignored or flagged. Aim for about 10–15 a day.

## Step 4: Amazon keywords

KDP gives you **7 keyword boxes** (up to 50 characters each). Your listing sheet already has 7. These 10 are long-tail alternatives to swap in, including **American spellings** ("coloring", "stocking stuffer"), because Amazon.com shoppers search that way:

1. `christmas coloring book for toddlers 1-3`
2. `toddler christmas coloring book big simple`
3. `stocking stuffer for toddlers 2 year old boy girl`
4. `easy christmas coloring pages for preschoolers`
5. `big picture christmas coloring book for kids`
6. `christmas crayon activity book for 2 3 year olds`
7. `advent activity for toddlers christmas colouring`
8. `santa snowman reindeer coloring pages for kids`
9. `christmas colouring book for nursery children`
10. `first crayons coloring book christmas toddler`

Suggested 7: numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 9. That gives a mix of American and British spellings and doesn't repeat words already in the title.

Don't use other authors' names, brands, "free", "bestseller" or "new" in keywords.
